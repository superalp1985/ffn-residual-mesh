# Iteration 66: Exact Mixed-Format Down Projections

Date: 2026-09-07

## Scope

Added exact Q5_K and IQ4_XS down kernels and a shared projection factory.
The existing Q4_K gate/up artifact is unchanged. This does not establish
general mixed-format gate/up support, complete-model residency, or generation.

The grouped Q5_K decoder uses the upstream affine scale/min formula and its
separate high-bit plane. IQ4_XS preserves the original nonlinear quantizer.
Neither path requantizes weights or drops channels.

Reference checked against local gguf-py and llama.cpp:
`vendor/llama.cpp/ggml/src/ggml-quants.c`, `dequantize_row_q5_K`.
Upstream source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-quants.c

The new runs pass the numerical gate, but DO NOT reproduce the earlier
latency. CPU base time and event gaps are materially higher. The cause is
not isolated; do not attribute this to DMA, power state, or background load
without a controlled measurement. Preserve both sets of observations.

zero additional residual-weight upload bytes after initialization.
